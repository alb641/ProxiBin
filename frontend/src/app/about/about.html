About Us

The team connecting Watchung residents with their haulers.

Our Story

ProxiBin started as a project by a team of NJIT students who wanted to make trash and recycling pickup easier to keep up with. Residents often don't know when their truck is coming and reporting a missed pickup can mean phone calls and waiting. We built ProxiBin to give residents and haulers one simple place to stay connected.

What We Care About

Clear Updates

Residents should always know when their truck is on the way.

Easy Reporting

Reporting a missed pickup or damaged bin should take seconds, not phone calls.

Built for Watchung

We work with the haulers residents already use: X-Cel Carting, Grand Sanitation and Interstate Waste.
