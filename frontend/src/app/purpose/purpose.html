Our Purpose

Making trash and recycling pickup simple, clear and connected.

The Problem

No Updates

Residents don't know when their truck is coming or if it's running late.

Hard to Report

Reporting a missed pickup or damaged bin usually means phone calls and waiting.

No Follow-Up

After reporting a problem, residents often never hear back about what happened.

How ProxiBin helps

For Residents

Follow your truck live, get alerts when it's close, report issues in seconds and see replies from your hauler.

For Haulers

See residents, tickets and truck activity in one place and reply to issues quickly.

Our Goal

We want every Watchung resident to feel in the know about their pickup and every hauler to have an easy way to stay connected with the people they serve. Better communication means fewer missed pickups and less frustration for everyone.
