Frequently Asked Question

What is ProxiBin?

ProxiBin helps residents stay informed about thier waste collection through pickup updates, alerts and easy issue reporting - all in one place.

Is ProxiBin my waste hauler?

No. ProxiBin does not replace your waste hauler. It connects residents with information about their existing waste collection service and provides an easier way to stay informed and report collection issuses.

Is ProxiBin available in my area?

ProxiBin is currently being piloted in Watchung, New Jersey. We plan to expand to additional communities in the future.

Do I choose my waste hauler through ProxiBin?

No. ProxiBin is designed for residents who already have a waste hauler. During setup, you select the hauler that currently services your residence.

Can I change my pickup schedule?

Your pickup schedule is based on the service associated with your residence. During onboarding, you can confirm or provide your current garbage and recycling schedule.

What can I reprot through ProxiBin?

Residents can report collection issues such as missed or late pickups, damaged bins, incomplete collection and other garbage or recycling service concerns.

Does ProxiBin charge residents?

The core ProxiBin resident experience is intended to be free for residents.

Is ProxiBin connected directly to my waste hauler?

The current ProxiBin pilot uses a prototype environment and simulated collection information. Future versions may support direct integrations with waste haulers.

I have a problem with my pickup. Who should I contact?

You can use ProxiBin to document and report your collection issue. For urgent or immediate service concerns, you may also contact your existing waste hauler directly.

How can I contact ProxiBin?

Use the inquiry form on this page to contact us. Select the inquiry type that best matches your question, including Resident Support, Waste Hauler Partnership, Municipality/Community Inquiry, Product Feedback, Report a Problem or General Inquiry.
