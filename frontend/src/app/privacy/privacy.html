Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Last updated: October 2026

ProxiBin is currently a capstone start-up project developed by a team of NJIT students. During the capstone, truck locations and collection updates are simulated.

Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address and password or the basic profile information shared when you sign in with Google or Microsoft.
  • Resident information: your name, address, phone number, whether you rent or own your home and confirmation that you are 18 or older.
  • Service information: your waste hauler, your garbage and recycling pickup days and your recycling frequency.
  • Preferences: whether you want to receive email alerts.
  • Reports: information you provide when you report a collection issue.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To confirm that ProxiBin is available at your address.
  • To show your pickup schedule and collection updates.
  • To send email alerts, if you turn them on.
  • To let your hauler see and respond to issues you report.
  • To improve ProxiBin.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with your selected hauler when needed to handle your pickups and reports with the service providers that host and run ProxiBin or when required by law.

4. How We Protect Your Information

Your information is stored securely and access rules make sure each resident can only see their own information. No system is completely secure but we work to keep your information safe

5. Your Choices

  • You can turn email alerts on or off.
  • You can ask us to update or delete your information through our Contact page.

6. Age Requirement

ProxiBin is only for residents who are 18 years of age or older.

Terms of Service

1. Using ProxiBin

By creating an account you confirm that you are 18 or older and that the information you provide is accurate.

2. ProxiBin Is Not Your Waste Hauler

ProxiBin does not provide garbage or recycling collection. You waste hauler is responsible for your service. Pickup times and truck locations shown in ProxiBin are estimates and may not always be exact.

3. Your Account

You are responsible for keeping your password safe and for activity on your account.

4. Acceptable Use

Please do no submit false reports, misuse ProxiBin or try to access other residents' information.

5. Capstone Project

ProxiBin is a capstone start-up project and is provided as is. Features may change and the service may be paused or ended at any time. ProxiBin is not responsible for missed pickups or for decisions made based on estimated times.
